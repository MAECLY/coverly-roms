> **TL;DR.** "Coverly ROMs", its wordmark and the Coverly mark are unregistered trademarks that belong to MAECLY (the founder, Miguel Angel Esparza Calero); see also the repository's [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md). Refer to us, link to us, write about us — that is all welcome. Do not use the name or the mark to imply our endorsement of your product or service. Forks must use a different name.

This policy explains how the Coverly ROMs name, wordmark and mark may and may not be used. It is independent of the [AGPL-3.0 licence](./license.md) on the source code: copyright and trademark are different rights. The one place the two meet is an additional term under section 7(e) of the AGPL, stated in [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md): the licence grants no rights under trademark law to these names and marks beyond reasonable and customary use in describing where the work came from (for example, "a fork of Coverly ROMs").

## Why a trademark policy

The AGPL-3.0 licenses the **source code**; apart from the section 7(e) term above, it says nothing about names and marks. Without an explicit policy:

- Someone could ship a malware-laced fork branded "Coverly ROMs". The app writes to people's SD cards and game libraries, so a trusted name matters.
- Others could imply we endorse their products, ROM packs or art packs.
- Confusion in the market could fragment the user community.

## What's covered

| Asset | Status |
|---|---|
| The name **"Coverly ROMs"** | Unregistered trademark of MAECLY |
| The wordmark (`Coverly` + `ROMs`) | Unregistered trademark of MAECLY |
| The Coverly mark (the app icon) | Unregistered trademark of MAECLY. The mark is being designed; until it ships the app uses the wordmark only. |

We have not registered "Coverly ROMs" with any trademark office. We may do so as the project matures. Even unregistered, common-law protections apply.

## Permitted uses (no permission needed)

- **Referring to Coverly ROMs by name** in articles, videos, guides, forum posts, comparisons and reviews.
- **Linking to** the GitHub repository, including with the wordmark if it is clearly a link.
- **Saying "uses Coverly ROMs"** or "art added with Coverly ROMs" when that is true, in a descriptive way.
- **Distributing unmodified Coverly ROMs** (binaries we released) under our name and mark, as we shipped them.
- **Educational use**: tutorials that teach how to use Coverly ROMs, with screenshots.

These are forms of **nominative fair use**: using the name to refer to the thing it identifies, without implying sponsorship.

## Uses requiring permission

Open an issue tagged `trademark` or write to hola@maecly.com first for:

- Branded merchandise.
- Domain names or social accounts containing "coverly" that could be confused with official ones.
- Use in the name of another product ("Coverly ROMs Plus" sold by a third party). We will generally decline these.

## Forbidden uses

- **Naming a fork "Coverly ROMs"** or anything confusingly similar ("Coverly ROMs Lite", "Coverly X", "CoverlyROMs Pro"). Forks must rebrand.
- **Using the name or mark to imply official endorsement** of a third-party product, ROM set, art pack or service.
- **Bundling ROMs, BIOS files or copyrighted art with our name or mark**, or presenting Coverly ROMs as a source of games. Coverly ROMs ships none.
- **Using the name to mislead users** about the source of a download (for example a site distributing modified binaries under our name).
- **Distorting or recolouring the mark**, or compositing it into your own logo.

## Forks

If you fork Coverly ROMs (which the AGPL-3.0 permits) and **distribute** the fork:

- **Rename it** and **replace the mark** with your own identity. [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md) lists every place in the repository where the name and identifiers live (product name, bundle identifier, app-data folder triple, updater key, package names, interface copy, icons).
- **Use your own updater key and endpoint.** A fork that keeps ours cannot publish updates its builds accept, and its users would look for our releases.
- **Be clear about the relationship** in your README: "This is a fork of Coverly ROMs vX.Y.Z. Original project: https://github.com/MAECLY/coverly-roms."
- **Add your own copyright notice** for your changes.

A personal patchset you do not distribute needs no rename.

## Reporting trademark issues

- **A fork or product using our name:** open an issue tagged `trademark`.
- **A site impersonating Coverly ROMs or distributing malware under its name:** use the private channels in [`SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md).

## Reservation of rights

We may update this policy; changes are announced in the CHANGELOG. We enforce our marks proportionately: a friendly request for honest mistakes, stronger action only against bad-faith use.
